\subsection*{Remnants of the Great Hand}

These are spells that once were written in Gaub's Grimoire$^1$\footnotetext[1]{\href{https://lostpages.co.uk/presskit/sheet.php?p=book_of_gaub}{The Book of Guab by Lost Pages}}, but as Hand's power dwindled -- they were lost to time.

\paragraph{The one on the pulse.} To cast a curse, fill a leather bag with rusty nails and put something with corporeal traces of the victim in it.
Pick one die. It is locked within a spell while it lasts. 
In order for curse to complete the victim must touch the prepared bag.

You can, at any time, exploit the curse as a spell.
Pick one die, shake the bag, roll the die locked in the spell -- victim takes that amount of damage. 

\paragraph{The one trailing letters.} TODO: Perform a ritual, it kills you, with your last dying breath you utter a word that bind itself to an object.
One who interacts with the object hears your voice. You keep your personality and memories while bound. 
If you convince a person to perform a ritual -- you possess their body, while they become bound to the object.

\paragraph{The one pointing the way.} Bury your hands and feet under of a road, trail or passage. 
Pick any number of dice. For each die which rolled its maximum value -- ask a question, the answer has as many words as the value rolled.
For each other die -- ask a question the answer will be yes, no or silence.

\paragraph{The one under floorboards.} You turn a hallway behind the door infinite for a duration of the spell. 
Anybody who enters is immediately lost even if the door is open. If group of people enters the are together as long as they can see each other. 
The hallway is can be just straight, but upon wish of the caster it can bend, have curves and appear finite at first. 

Pick any number of dice and calculate the sum of their values: thats how long spell lasts in hours. 
You can support the spell at twilight by picking more dice.

\paragraph{The one gnarled to the bone.} You poor vengeful power into your body. 
Once blade punctures your skin that power finds the living being that caused that harm and returns the favour.

Pick any number of dice and calculate the sum of their values. That is the amount of damage you can return. 
Each time a creature you can see reduces your \texttt{Vigor} by dealing damage -- it loses that much \texttt{Vigor} as well.
Each time that damage causes an \texttt{Injury} that creature receives one also.

The spell lasts while you are awake. 

\paragraph{The one that is not there.} Pick any number of dice, then pick a die with value equal to that number. 
Until the spell ends one can't form memories about you. 
You can't be targeted by attacks or spells and your attacks ignore \texttt{Guard}, but you can only attack alone.

The spell breaks if number of creatures that would perceive you without it surpasses the value on a dice you picked last for this spell. 
Spell lasts 15 minutes for each dice you picked for it.

\paragraph{The one bottling tears.} You touch a victim and a jar. Pick any number of dice that sum to target's \texttt{Vigor} or greater. 

After several horrifying seconds they start liquefying and realize that they are being sucked into that jar you hold.
They sense and hear things happening outside of the jar, but can't see. When the jar is broken -- the escape and restore themselves to original state. 
They do not experience hunger or thirst while inside.
