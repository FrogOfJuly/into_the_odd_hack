\subsection*{Blood magic}

Once widely practiced, lead to several wars between common people and magic wielders. Nearly drove craft of spellweaving to extinction. 
Forbidden ever since. 

Those who practice it considered outlaws by folks with and without arcane abilities alike. 
Somewhat tolerated, but not trusted by witches who sometimes suspect that they are next on the hunting list.

\paragraph{I sacrifice my flesh.} You pull out a knife and with a swift motion cut your skin open. 
Warmth of blood and metallic smell fills you with overwhelming sensation of returning power. 

Roll \texttt{d8} as if you attacked yourself with a knife. You can't influence that \texttt{d8} roll with \texttt{Focus} and \texttt{Stress}.

Pick a single die that has its rolled value equal to damage dealt. Take that much \texttt{Vigor} damage and restore that many spellweaving dice.
This damage is dealt directly to \texttt{Vigor}, causes \texttt{Scar} and can result in an \texttt{Injury}. 



\paragraph{Your body is wasted on you. } Choose a target, roll \texttt{d20} and add their \texttt{Vigor}, this value represents how hard they resist you consuming their strength. You can't influence that \texttt{d20} roll with \texttt{Focus} and \texttt{Stress}.
 
Pick any number of dice, add them together, add your \texttt{Vigor} that's your drain on them. 
 
If your drain is higher than their ability to resist -- they lose \texttt{Vigor} equal to the difference and you restore as many spellweaving dice as much \texttt{Vigor} was lost this way in any of your pools.

If they withstand your drain -- you lose \texttt{Vigor} equal to the difference.
