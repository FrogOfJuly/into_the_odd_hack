\subsection*{Fear \& Hope}

Spend hope as focus for yourself or stress for friendly NPCs. 

Fear is spent by Referee as stress for your enemies or to reflect the opposition to player actions.

\newcolumn

\subsection*{Before breakfast}

\paragraph{Restoration} happens here. 
If nothing special is going on -- restore 3 points distributed among your virtues, decrease stress by 1, restore focus. 
Usually, spellweavers restore their spellcasting dice at this time, see \pageref*{sec:spellweaving}.

\paragraph{Maladies} trigger in the mornings, so watch out for them.

\subsection*{During the day} 

There are a lot of things happening during the day, but the most important one is rest. 

\paragraph{Short rest} spans for an hour, you can restore your focus that way. 
You can restore focus that amounts up to your maximum through short rests between two overnight sleeps.

\paragraph{Healing an injury} takes an effort from both the one healing and the one being healed, see page \pageref*{sec:healing}. 
Without painkillers anyone can endure a single healing attempt per day.

\paragraph{Focus \& Stress} can be restored through sheer force of will. 
You can sacrifice you spirit to reduce stress and clarity to replenish you focus, see page \pageref*{sec:recovery}.

\subsection*{Before bed}

See if place is safe, overnight sleep can be dangerous without night watch. 

\paragraph{Dealing with trauma} is done in you sleep with resources that you ended your day with, see page \pageref*{sec:healing}.

\paragraph{Remedies} are usually used at supper, so open up that wine bottle.
