\subsection*{Rolls}

\paragraph{Saves} are rolled when overcoming a challenge posed against you that you might fail. 
There are three possible saves, one for each virtue: Vigor save, Clarity save and Spirit save.

You roll d20 and if you rolled below or equal to relevant virtues -- you succeed. Otherwise the save is failed.

Failed save does not necessary determines failure of the action, but there surely will be negative consequences.

Rolling a natural 20 increases your Fear.

\paragraph{Damage rolls}

Primary target for using your focus and stress. Dice come from your weapons, feats and other sources.

For each 1 you rolled during damage roll after focus and stress are applied, your company gains one Fear.
For each 1 your enemy rolled, you gain one Hope.

\paragraph{Spellwaeving rolls}

Determine the dice that will be used in the spell from any spellweaving pool available for you. Apply Focus, 
apply Stress, choose and resolve the spell, rethread the arcane.

\paragraph{Luck}

Sometimes things are just out of your control. You can't impact this roll by definition, so no stress or focus is applicable.
For fifty-fifty rolls use any dice, lower half of values is for fails, the rest is success.

\paragraph{Table rolls}

There are a lot of rolls that have associated tables: scars, afflictions and more. 
Those are usually can't be influenced by stress and focus.

\paragraph{Recieving damage}

Generally, attack against you goes through the same steps, but some are ommited for simplicity. 
You use your focus through \emph{Defense} mechanism and spend Guard to dodge damage. 

If your guard is unchanged after receiving damage to Vigor you roll for a scar. 
Roll the damage die of the attack and add the amount of occupied injury slots to value rolled. 

Choose the corresponding option from the table on page \pageref*{sec:injuries}.

If the damage you received is greater than 4, but less than half of your Vigor you gain a Severe Injury. 
If the damage is greater of equal to half of your Vigor you gain Lethal injury. 

Roll affliction on page \pageref*{sec:injuries}.

\subsection*{Spellcasting}

Both witches and spellweavers can cast spells. Casting a spell in combat takes an action and threatens fatigue.
